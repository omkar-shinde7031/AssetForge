# AssetForge
<h2>AssetForge is a fast, lightweight tool for organizing, processing, and managing your digital assets in one place</h2>
<br>
AssetForge is a web app for keeping track of IT hardware: who has which laptop, what's sitting in the storage room, and what's about to run out.

I built it because inventory in most small teams ends up in a spreadsheet that nobody trusts. Someone takes a monitor, forgets to write it down, and three weeks later nobody knows where it went. AssetForge tries to make the boring part (logging who took what) quick enough that people actually do it

<h2>What you can do with it</h2>

<h3>1. See everything at a glance.</h3> The dashboard shows total assets, how many are in stock, how many people are active, and the total value of your hardware. There's also a low stock panel that tells you when something monitors, RAM, servers, etc. has dropped below its minimum, and a live chart of assigned vs. in stock vs. under repair

<h3>2. Receive new stock.</h3> When a delivery arrives, the Intake Panel lets you log the vendor, the packing slip or PO number, and exactly what came in: monitors, keyboards, headsets, docking stations, phones and so on

<h3>3. Export reports.</h3> The Reports page shows spend by vendor and assets per department, and you can download the asset list as a CSV

<h3>4. Handle onboarding requests.</h3> Pick whether it's a new joiner, a lab assistant or an existing employee, choose their department and role, and it fills in a starter kit for you.

<h3>5. Manage assets and people.</h3> Every asset gets a tag like LAP-0003, a serial number, vendor, location, cost and status. You can search and filter by category or status. The Employees page shows each person and how many assets they currently hold
